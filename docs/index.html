ScreenJot

Fast screenshots with scribbles and quick GIF recordings, from your Mac menu bar.

Download for macOS

Free and open source · macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel · all releases

Shortcuts

⌘⇧4Take a screenshot, then draw on it in place
⌘⇧5Record a GIF (15 fps, up to 30 seconds)
TabCycle the target: Region → Window → Full Screen
⏎Copy the result to the clipboard
⇧⏎Save As…
escCancel
⌘ZUndo a stroke

While ScreenJot is running it takes over the native ⌘⇧4 and ⌘⇧5 shortcuts. Quitting restores them.

Installing

  1. Open the DMG and drag ScreenJot into Applications.
  2. ScreenJot is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch. Open it once, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. This is only needed once.
    Or skip the dialog from Terminal: 
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/ScreenJot.app
  3. Grant Screen Recording permission when asked, then relaunch ScreenJot from the menu bar.
